## Where I'm at right now

I spent the last years on the hiring side of IT - reading CVs, running screens, deciding who moves forward. Somewhere in there I got more interested in the work than in the interviews about the work, so in June 2026 I started learning security properly.

Five certifications later I'm working through the SOC Level 1 track, solving a CTF room most days and writing up every single one. Detection, log analysis and incident response are where I want to end up. Target is a blue team role by the end of 2026.

I'm early in this and not pretending otherwise. What's here is just the record of the work, kept in public and dated, so you can see how it's going.

Every room I solve gets a full writeup: recon, each step of the chain, what worked, what I got wrong, what I'd do differently. Most of these are offensive rooms - breaking things is the fastest way I've found to understand what defending them actually needs to catch.

|---|---|---|---|

| 06 | Overheard at Breakfast | OSINT · Easy | How much a single careless post gives away |
